The 6 Rules

Movement Phase-adapted
One structured workout (45–60 min) + daily outdoor walk. Max 3 HIIT sessions per week. During luteal: reduce intensity, favour strength or yoga.
Eat to nourish
3 meals, no skipping. Prioritise protein (1.6–2g/kg), healthy fats, complex carbs. No calorie deficit. No alcohol for 75 days.
3 litres of water
Herbal teas count. Limit caffeine to 1 cup before noon. Add electrolytes if training hard.
Read 10 pages
Real book only. No screens 30 min before bed — cortisol and melatonin are linked.
Stress practice Cortisol-critical
10 min daily: breathwork, meditation, or journaling. 7–9 hours sleep. Cold exposure optional — follicular phase only.
Track your cycle The key one
Log energy, mood, discharge, and symptoms daily. Adjust training by phase. Note any cycle length changes — they're data.
